Where this site stands

Most of what follows presumes one account of the condition: that the hard flaccid state is a sustained contraction of penile smooth muscle, driven by excessive sympathetic outflow via the hypogastric nerve, generally following a peripheral nerve injury. That is the pelvic/pudendal-hypogastric reflex proposed by Goldstein and Komisaruk, and it is the most developed model anyone has put forward. It is also a hypothesis. Penile sympathetic outflow has never been measured in a patient, and no treatment derived from the theory has been tested against a control.

The whole literature runs to about twenty items, most of them case reports, conference abstracts and correspondence. Much of what is written here is uncertain, and some of it is speculation, labeled as such wherever it appears. As the evidence changes, the affected articles will be revised and the change recorded in News and updates. What is here is an attempt to set out the condition and the evidence for it accurately, including the places where the honest answer is that nobody knows.
